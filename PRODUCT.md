# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React, TypeScript, and Vite for the static interface; standard-library Python in a Pyodide worker for search.

## Users
CS246/346 students and instructors exploring uninformed and informed state-space search. A first-time visitor can play without reading about algorithms first.

## Product Purpose
Play or build a Rush Hour puzzle, observe BFS and A* on the same position, and replay a legal solution move by move.

## Product Principles
The board explains the action. Separate search expansions from solution moves. Explain every metric in plain language. Keep the first puzzle playable while the solver starts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Support keyboard, touch, narrow phones, reduced motion, and state markers that remain readable without color.
